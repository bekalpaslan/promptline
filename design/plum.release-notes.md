### Themes

- **Plum**, November's theme and the first one picked by vote, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: an aubergine ground, soft lilac-grey text and rose as the one accent. The light side is the same hues on blush white.
- Plum fills Paste, Apply and Import with rose and dark text in dark mode, and with deep rose and white text in light.
- Controls have the softest corners of any theme, 12 px, and the search boxes match.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
