### Themes

- **Graphite**, November's theme and the first one picked by vote, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: greys only, in both modes, so the only colour left on screen is colour that means something: errors, warnings, placeholders and your tags.
- Graphite fills Paste, Apply and Import with near-white on dark and near-black on light.
- Tags are outlines in their hue with no fill.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
