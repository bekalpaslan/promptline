### Themes

- **Lagoon**, November's theme and the first one picked by vote, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: a deep teal ground with coral as the one accent. The light side is the same pair on pale seafoam.
- Lagoon fills Paste, Apply and Import with coral and dark text in dark mode, and with deep coral and white text in light.
- Controls and the search boxes have 10 px corners, and tags carry a faint edge of their hue.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
