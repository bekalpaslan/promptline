### Themes

- **Moss**, November's theme and the first one picked by vote, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: a green-black ground, sage-tinted text and lichen green as the one accent. The light side is the same greens on pale paper.
- Moss fills Paste, Apply and Import with lichen and dark text in dark mode, and with deep green and white text in light.
- Controls and the search boxes have 6 px corners.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
