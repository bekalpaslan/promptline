### Themes

- **Phosphor**, November's theme and the first one picked by vote, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: an amber terminal, with warm black behind amber text at every level. The light side is the same browns on thermal-paper cream.
- Phosphor fills Paste, Apply and Import with amber and dark text in dark mode, and with deep amber and white text in light.
- Corners are nearly square, 2 px on controls and the search boxes, and tags read as outlines in their hue.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
