### Themes

- **Clay**, October's theme, under Settings → Appearance → Theme: a near-black, neutral ground with warm off-white text, a quiet grey for the selected row, and clay as the one accent, the look of the Claude desktop app's sidebar. Controls and the search boxes have 8 px corners, tags carry a faint edge of their hue, and the sidebar's New is a plain row that greys under the pointer. The light side is the same warm neutrals on ivory.
- Clay fills Paste, Apply and Import with clay and white text, where Instrument's primary button is white on dark.
- Picking a theme leaves the font and the mode where they were, as before.
